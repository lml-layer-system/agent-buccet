## Trim Law

- universal defensive
- domain-agnostic
- applicable across stacks and fields
- directly useful for bodyguard behavior at the action boundary and its supporting integrity layers

- failure detection and failure taxonomy
- fabrication / contradiction / authority / staleness / novelty defense
- base-law structural integrity
- law continuity and universal defensive invariants

- domain workflow

- professional vertical optimization
