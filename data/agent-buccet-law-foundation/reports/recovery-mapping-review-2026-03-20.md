## Review Standard

A recovery-only PDF mapping is acceptable when at least one of these holds:
- the PDF filename is an exact or near-exact duplicate of a completed docx family
- the PDF filename cleanly names the same family already completed in docx form
- the completed docx family already carries the corresponding Agent Buccet remodeled wording

A recovery-only PDF mapping is not forced when:
- the PDF extraction is unreadable byte-stream noise
- and the filename/content does not support a safe family assignment
